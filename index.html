Relaxing History

Last updated: September 2026

Relaxing History is private software used by its developer to produce and publish long-form, narrated history videos to a single YouTube channel, designed as calm background listening for falling asleep.

What This Is

This is not a public product, service, or platform. It is not made available for use by any third party or any other Google account - it is a private automation tool operated solely by its developer to manage one specific YouTube channel's content.

How It Works

The application researches and writes narration scripts, generates narration audio and visuals, assembles finished videos, and publishes them to the developer's own YouTube channel. It does not interact with, collect data from, or serve any third party.
